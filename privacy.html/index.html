그냥1분 Publishing Tool

An internal tool that sends our own finished short videos to our own TikTok drafts.

Effective 2026-09-11 Scopes: user.info.basic · video.upload

01 What this tool is

The 그냥1분 Publishing Tool is a private, internal script used by our own team to publish short videos we produce in-house. It takes a finished video file, attaches a title and hashtags we prepared in advance, and delivers it to the TikTok drafts (inbox) of the channel owner's own account. A person then opens the TikTok app, reviews the draft, and publishes it.

It is not a consumer product. It has no sign-up, no public interface, and no users other than the operators of the channels it posts to.

02 What we access, and why

PermissionWhat it gives usWhy we need it
user.info.basic Account identifier (open ID) and display name To confirm the video is being sent to the intended account before uploading
video.upload Ability to place a video file into the account's drafts To deliver the finished video. Publishing is done by the account owner in the TikTok app

We request no other permission. We do not read followers, comments, messages, analytics, or any other account data.

03 What we store

  • The TikTok access token and refresh token issued when the account owner authorizes the tool.
  • The account's open ID and display name.
  • The identifier returned for each upload, so we can check whether it arrived.

These are stored in a local configuration file on the operator's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server of ours, because we do not operate a server.

04 What we do not do

  • We do not collect information from TikTok viewers or from anyone who is not an operator.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share data with any third party.
  • We do not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling, or training models.
  • We do not run analytics or tracking of any kind.

05 How long we keep it

Tokens are kept only while the tool is in use for that account. When an account stops using the tool, its tokens are deleted from the configuration file. Upload identifiers are kept as a record for up to 12 months and then removed.

06 Withdrawing access

The account owner can revoke this tool at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions. Revoking immediately stops all access. On request we will also delete the stored tokens for that account; contact us at the address below.

07 Children

The tool is used only by the operators of our own channels, all of whom are adults. It is not directed at children and collects nothing from them.

08 Changes

If what the tool accesses ever changes, this page is updated and the effective date at the top changes with it.

09 Contact

Questions about this policy or a deletion request: ferraric.lec16@gmail.com

그냥1분 Publishing Tool

Terms for the internal tool that publishes our finished videos to our own TikTok account.

Effective 2026-09-11 Internal use only

01 Scope

These terms cover the 그냥1분 Publishing Tool ("the Tool"), a private script operated by our team. The Tool is used only by the operators of the TikTok accounts it posts to. It is not offered to the public and has no registration.

02 What the Tool does

The Tool takes a video file we produced, attaches a title and hashtags we prepared, and shows the operator a confirmation screen: the name of the TikTok account that will receive the post, a preview of the video, and the full caption. On that screen the operator chooses the privacy level (no value is pre-selected), decides whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed (all start off), and declares commercial content if any (off by default). The file is sent to TikTok only after the operator presses Post.

The Tool never publishes unattended. It requests user.info.basic, video.publish and video.upload. It has no scheduler and no unattended mode: every post needs a person on the confirmation screen to choose the settings and press Post.

03 Authorization

Before the Tool can deliver anything, the account owner must authorize it through TikTok's own login screen. Authorization can be withdrawn at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions. Once withdrawn, the Tool can no longer access that account.

04 Content responsibility

All videos published by the Tool are produced by us and remain our responsibility. We are responsible for making sure each video complies with TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service before it is published. The operator who presses Post on the confirmation screen is the person confirming the content is ready.

05 Acceptable use

  • The Tool is used only for accounts we own or are explicitly authorized to operate.
  • It is not used to post on behalf of anyone who has not authorized it.
  • It is not used for spam, bulk automated posting to unrelated accounts, or engagement manipulation.
  • It is not used to bypass any TikTok limit, review, or restriction.

06 Relationship to TikTok

The Tool uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API and is bound by TikTok's Developer Terms of Service. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of TikTok. If TikTok's terms and these terms ever conflict, TikTok's terms apply.

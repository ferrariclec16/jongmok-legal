Internal publishing tool

그냥1분 Publishing Tool

A private script our team uses to deliver the short videos we produce to our own TikTok drafts. Every post is reviewed and published by a person in the TikTok app.

How it works

  • 01We finish a video. Script, narration, footage and captions are produced by our team, not by this tool.
  • 02The tool attaches the title and hashtags we wrote and sends the file to the authorized account's TikTok drafts.
  • 03A person opens the TikTok app, reviews the draft, chooses visibility and comment settings, and publishes it.

Permissions it uses

ScopeUsed for
user.info.basicConfirming the video is going to the intended account before upload
video.uploadPlacing the finished file into that account's drafts
It does not request video.publish. The tool cannot post to a public feed on its own — publishing is always a human step taken inside the TikTok app.

Who uses it

Only the operators of the channels it posts to. There is no sign-up, no public interface, and no third-party users. It reads no follower, comment, message or analytics data.
